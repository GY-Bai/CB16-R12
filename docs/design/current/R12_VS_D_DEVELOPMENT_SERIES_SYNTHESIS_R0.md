## 3. What the series supports

The series supports only a process-level conclusion: **the currently exposed single-asset development material has been adaptively reused across multiple narrow diagnostics, and none of the tested local interventions earned its preregistered promotion.**

Repeated weak positive point estimates are retained. In particular, they justify neither deletion of the larger CB16 design nor promotion of this probe family.

## 4. What the series does not support

R0-R9 do not establish that markets lack learnable information, that N0 is globally inadequate, that nonlinear methods cannot work, that 64h/1h is universally optimal or wrong, that account-aware policies cannot work, or that final CB16 is infeasible.

The series also does not identify a unique root cause.

## 5. Stopping rule for this narrow development family

Do not continue serial one-factor micro-screens on the same exposed BTCUSDT January-March 2020 material merely by changing another threshold, deadband, lambda, context, horizon, seed set, or similarly local knob.

A new VS-D family should require at least one explicit admission basis:

1. a materially different tested object required by current R12 design (for example a genuinely account-aware/continuing-interaction or representation-learning object rather than another frozen linear-probe variant);
2. a preregistered interaction hypothesis that cannot be answered by the exhausted local screens;
3. a separately reviewed historical-data role assignment enabling a legitimate transfer/robustness design on data not already adapted to this series; or
4. new evidence from another authorized component that creates a scope-matched dependency.

This stopping rule blocks serial rescue, not scientific progress.
